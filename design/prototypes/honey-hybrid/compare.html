Honey hybrid

The old live design's warm honey palette and character, with the Blue Ribbon glow direction's polish. Three hybrids to choose by eye, next to plain Blue Ribbon and the old design. Every hybrid and Blue Ribbon frame is the live prototype: tab and type in it. On the round screen, type rhythm and press Enter for the correct moment, or anything else to ring the bell.

Phone width

a. Hive old palette throughout

The old live site's colours kept almost exactly: charcoal #16130f and card #211c15 in dark, cream #faf3e3 in light, honey #e8a63d as the one accent. The glow is gentle, close to the old 7% wallpaper, so it reads as the old site grown up rather than a new look.

  • The title is in honey again, with the old tagline: "Hear it. Spell it. Beat the clock."
  • The old difficulty ramp (sage, olive, amber, burnt orange, honey, rust) comes back as a swatch beside each tier.
  • "Hear it again" is the old honey-outlined pill. Place ribbons are gold, silver and bronze.

b. Honey and ribbon honey leads, a little blue

Honey carries everything a player acts on: buttons, focus, the field, the timer. Ribbon blue appears only on the award marks, so it reads as the prize: the rosette's ribbon and centre ring, the first-place ribbon, the winner line, the "(you)" tag and the "Your word is" cue. The glow is a step richer than a.

c. Split honey glow dark, old flat light

Dark takes the full Blue Ribbon glow intensity, translated to amber: a deep cocoa black with a warm bloom and bright honey cell edges. Light is the old live site as it was: flat cream, the honeycomb at low alpha across the whole page, no bloom, no vignette and no moving light. Only the UI polish is new in light.

2. The answer moments in each hybrid

Correct: the field lights up green, a tick appears, the placard glows and +14 floats up. Missed: the bell rings, the field gives one short shake, the edge turns red and the word is shown.
