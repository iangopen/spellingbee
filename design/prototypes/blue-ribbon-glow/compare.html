1. Background variations

The same home screen with three backgrounds (no bee). The moving light animates only transform and opacity, pauses when the tab is hidden, and is removed under reduced motion, which leaves exactly the static version.

Static glow

Gradient, glowing cell edges, cells fading toward the edges. No motion.

Slow shimmer

A soft band of brighter cells crosses every 18 seconds, then rests. It ramps in slowly and never flashes.

Reactive glow

A soft light follows the mouse. On touch it rests behind the focused control and glides when focus moves.

2. Bee or no bee

No bee: the rosette carries a tick, and the avatars are placards with the eight glyphs. Bee: an original blue bee mascot sits on the hero panel and inside the rosette, and every contestant is a bee (the queen wears a crown, the drone has big eyes, the wasp is slim). The avatar keys don't change; only the art does.

3. The round and its two moments

Mid-answer

The field glows on focus. Try typing and pressing Enter.

Correct

The field lights up green, the placard glows, and +14 floats up.

Missed

The bell rings, the field gives one short shake, and the word is shown.

4. Checks

  • Contrast: every text element, control edge and focus ring is measured against the worst pixel behind it, with the glow at its peak, in both themes. 152 pairs, 0 failing: contrast.md.
  • Focus rings: measured in the real focused state. Each ring sits in a 7px solid moat, so no glow ever touches it.
  • Reduced motion: the shimmer and reactive variants render identically to the static background (8 of 8 comparisons).
  • Phone: at 390px with the CPU slowed 4×, the median frame is 16.7 ms, no frame is over 25 ms, only transform and opacity animate, and all background animation pauses in a hidden tab: perf.txt.
  • No yellow and no letter layout: checks.txt.
